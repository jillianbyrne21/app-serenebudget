Simple, stress-free weekly household budgeting, ring-fenced event vaults, and tax-back tracking.

About the App

Life in Excel is a practical, easy-to-use budgeting and cashflow application designed for households, families, and sole traders. Built on a proven 52-week rolling ledger system, it helps users protect their finances without complicated banking links.

  • Day-One Ring-Fenced Vaults: Automatically locks away funds for Christmas, birthdays, holidays, and annual insurance renewals so you never accidentally overspend.
  • Multi-Earner & Sporadic Income Support: Harmonises weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and irregular business income into a clear weekly cashflow plan.
  • Medical & Revenue Tax-Back Log: Tracks out-of-pocket medical and dental expenses to simplify annual tax return claims.
  • Multi-Language Support: Available in English, Polish, and additional European languages.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Serene Budget ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and financial data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide and improve the app experience, including your email address for account login, subscription status, and the budget entries you choose to save within your profile. We do not connect to or scrape your private bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your financial logs are used strictly to calculate your personal budget projections, ring-fenced vaults, and calendar reminders. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Deletion & User Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated budget data at any time. To request data deletion, use the in-app account settings or email our support team directly.
